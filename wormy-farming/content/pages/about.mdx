## Why this site exists

Most people do not need another reminder that composting is good. They need a way to make it work in real life. That means clear guidance, realistic expectations, and a process that respects space, time, and confidence.

Wormy Farming approaches vermicomposting as an everyday household system. It can help gardeners improve soil and help households reduce waste, but only if the process feels understandable enough to stick with.

## The editorial stance

This site is built around a simple idea: useful composting content should answer the questions people actually ask before they buy, start, or commit.

That means covering:

- what worm farming is good for
- how it compares with other food-waste options
- what beginners worry about
- what setup choices matter
- what results are realistic

We are not trying to romanticize a bin of worms. We are trying to remove confusion and make the tradeoffs visible.

## Who we are for

Wormy Farming is for three overlapping audiences:

- the organic gardener looking for stronger soil performance
- the urban household looking for a cleaner waste habit
- the family that wants a hands-on, teachable system

Those audiences share a common need: a composting method that feels guided instead of improvised.

## The advisory promise

Our stance is customer-side, not product-first. We want to help readers choose the right approach, avoid preventable mistakes, and understand where worm farming fits among other sustainable gardening solutions.

If worm farming is the right fit, we want that to be obvious. If it is not, we want the reasons to be equally clear.
