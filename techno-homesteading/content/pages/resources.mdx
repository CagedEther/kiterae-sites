## What belongs here

Techno Homesteading’s resource library is designed around decision-making. Instead of chasing volume, we focus on materials that help you move from idea to execution.

Planned resource types include:

- starter guides for common projects
- checklists for first-time planning
- seasonal planning worksheets
- tool comparisons and buying frameworks
- workflow guides for automation and monitoring
- reference hubs for digital homesteading resources

## A useful content system

The research behind this site points to a simple market gap: people want knowledge, tools, and support in one place. Resources are where that gap becomes concrete. They help readers decide what to do first, what to ignore, and how to measure progress.

## Topics we are building around

You will see resources organized around the main questions people are already asking:

- What are tech tools for homesteading?
- How do I automate a homestead without overcomplicating it?
- What is sustainable living technology in practice?
- Which smart farming solutions fit a small property?
- How do I plan a homestead before I buy more equipment?

## Why this matters

The strongest homesteading resources do more than inspire. They reduce uncertainty. They help people avoid wasted seasons, mismatched tools, and disconnected workflows.

That is the standard here: useful, selective, and built for action.
