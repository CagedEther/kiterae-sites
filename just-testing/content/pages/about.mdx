## Our point of view

Just testing exists because skincare is full of soft claims and vague comfort language. Coconut oil for dry skin is a good example: it is easy to sell as “natural,” but harder to make genuinely useful for people who care about texture, performance, and skin comfort.

We take the customer’s side. That means we care about the unglamorous questions: Does it feel greasy? Is it actually practical for daily use? What proof should exist before someone pays a premium?

## What we believe

A useful skincare brand should do three things at once: explain the ingredient, acknowledge the tradeoffs, and show where the formula earns trust.

In this category, that means being honest about the limits of commodity coconut oil while also recognizing why the ingredient remains compelling. It is familiar, widely understood, and still relevant to shoppers who want fewer synthetic inputs.

## Who this is for

This site is for people with dry skin, ingredient-conscious shoppers, and family buyers who want one solution that is simple enough to use and credible enough to keep using.

They are not looking for a miracle. They are looking for a product that fits real life.

## How we approach guidance

We prefer concrete comparisons over brand theater. We look at:

- texture and wearability
- the gap between natural and clinical positioning
- what a premium price needs to justify
- which proof points matter and which ones are just decoration

If a coconut oil formula can meet those standards, it deserves attention. If it cannot, it should be compared honestly against better-known moisturizers.
