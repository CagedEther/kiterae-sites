## Start here

If you are trying to decide whether coconut oil belongs in your dry-skin routine, you do not need more hype. You need a cleaner way to compare formulas.

This resources page collects the kinds of assets a careful buyer would actually use: plain-language explainers, usage guidance, comparison frameworks, and a shortlist of questions to ask before buying.

## What to ask before you buy

- Is this pure oil or a formulated product?
- How does it address greasiness?
- What makes the price higher than commodity coconut oil?
- Is there any testing behind the claim?
- Is the sourcing explained clearly?

## Why this matters

The category is full of products that sound similar but behave very differently. A few minutes of careful reading can save you from buying something that looks clean but feels inconvenient, or something clinical that never fits your preferences.

Use the resources here as a filter. If the brand cannot answer these questions well, it probably has not earned your trust yet.
