## What you will find here

This resource hub is meant to support the real decisions behind a solar project. Instead of generic inspiration, it focuses on the questions homeowners actually ask when the stakes get higher.

### Installation guides

Use these to understand the residential solar process from the first site check to installation day and system activation. The goal is to make the sequence less mysterious before you speak with installers.

### Financing explainers

Solar financing can change the economics more than most buyers expect. These resources break down loan structures, payment terms, and the practical tradeoffs between cash, loans, and other purchase paths.

### Quote review tools

If you already have proposals, this is where you would look for a quote checklist or analysis framework. The purpose is to normalize the offer, identify hidden assumptions, and reveal where one bid actually differs from another.

### Long-term planning guides

Solar should not be evaluated in isolation. Planning for batteries, EV charging, and future efficiency upgrades can help you avoid expensive rework later. These guides are built to help you think in five-year and ten-year terms.

## How to use this hub

If you are just starting, begin with installation basics. If you are comparing offers, jump to quote review. If you are thinking beyond the install, use the long-term planning material first.

The best resource is the one that helps you make the next decision with less guesswork.
