# Editorial Notes

- The source document includes unsupported claims such as projected market sizes, growth rates, clinical outcomes, fair-trade certification, dermatologist-tested status, user testimonials, before/after results, and specific brand performance statements. These were not carried into the site copy as factual claims without proof.
- The report’s suggested proof points should be treated as editorial-to-do items for future substantiation, not as present-tense claims on the site.
- The blog lead images were created as abstract editorial prompts rather than product-specific depictions, to avoid implying unverified product ownership or clinical results.
- A future version of the site would benefit from adding substantiated proof assets for non-comedogenic testing, sourcing documentation, and any dermatologist review before using stronger benefit claims.
