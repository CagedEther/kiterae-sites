The best solar decision usually comes from a few good resources used in the right order. This page is the hub.

Use these materials when you want to go deeper than a sales conversation but do not want to drown in random search results.

### What to expect here

- **Installation guides** that explain the solar process from first evaluation to final connection
- **Financing explainers** that show how loan terms, cash purchases, and other structures change the real cost
- **Quote comparison checklists** that help you spot differences in equipment, assumptions, and warranty coverage
- **Installer vetting prompts** that help you ask about service, support, and business stability
- **Electrification planning tools** for homeowners thinking about EV charging, batteries, or smart panels

### How to use the hub

1. Start with a basic installation guide if you are still early.
2. Move to financing if you are comparing total cost and monthly payment.
3. Review quote analysis if you already have proposals.
4. Use planning tools if your home energy needs will change over the next few years.

### What we are building toward

The content strategy here is designed around the real questions homeowners ask: what does solar cost, how do I compare installers, what financing is worth considering, and how do I avoid buying a system that feels outdated too soon.

That means the hub will continue to grow into a small library of decision support, not just blog posts. Think of it as the working set of materials you wish every installer had to present before asking you to sign.

If you are researching now, the next best step is simple: gather your quotes, note your future home plans, and use the resources here to compare them with more discipline.
